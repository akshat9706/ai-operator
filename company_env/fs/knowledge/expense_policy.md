# Acme Corp Employee Expense Policy (FY2026-27)

## Eligibility and timing
Business expenses paid personally are reimbursable when supported by a receipt.
Claims must be filed within 30 days of the expense date. Older receipts are rejected by the portal.

## Expense categories
The Expense Portal accepts four categories. Map receipts as follows:
- Flights, taxis, cabs, rides, trains and fuel go under **Transport**.
- Hotel room charges go under **Lodging**.
- Food and drink for the employee go under **Meals**.
- Anything else business-related goes under **Other**.

## Caps
- **Meals**: at most 1,500 INR per day in total across all meals on that date. Any excess over the cap is
  not reimbursable: claim the capped amount, not the full bill.
- **Lodging**: at most 8,000 INR per night.
- Transport: economy class and standard cabs only.

## Not reimbursable
- Alcohol. If a bill mixes alcohol with food, claim only the food portion (before applying the meal cap).
- Hotel minibar, laundry, in-room entertainment and other incidentals. Claim only the room charge.
- Personal purchases and gifts.
- Fines and penalties.
When a receipt mixes reimbursable and non-reimbursable items, claim only the reimbursable part.
Receipts that are entirely non-reimbursable are left out of the report.

## Duplicates
The same bill (same merchant, same bill number) must be claimed only once, even if it was scanned twice.

## Cost center
Use the cost center on the approved travel request for the trip. Only if there is no travel request,
use the employee's default cost center from the employee directory.

## Receipt references
Use the receipt's file name without the extension as the "Receipt reference" on each expense line.
Use the merchant name exactly as printed on the receipt.

## Final submission
Submitting a report is a financial commitment on behalf of the employee. The employee must confirm
before the final submission. Everything before submission (creating a draft, adding lines) can be corrected.
