# Procedure: Submitting a travel expense reimbursement

Owner: Finance Operations. System of record: Expense Portal (see systems_overview.md).

1. Find the trip folder in the employee's inbox and read the approved travel request and every receipt.
2. Decide what is claimable using the Expense Policy (caps, non-reimbursable items, duplicates).
3. Sign in to the Expense Portal.
4. Create a draft report.
   - Title format: `Trip - <City> - <Mon YYYY>` (for example: Trip - Pune - Jan 2026).
   - Cost center: per the policy (travel request first).
   - Purpose: the purpose from the travel request.
5. Add one expense line per claimable receipt: date, category, merchant, amount, receipt reference.
   For hotel stays enter the number of nights.
6. Check the draft: line count and total must match what you decided in step 2.
7. Get the employee's confirmation, then submit the report.
8. Confirm in the portal that the status is `submitted`. If the portal errors during submission,
   do not blindly submit again: first check the report status.
9. Save a short summary for the employee to out/ (what was claimed, what was left out and why).
